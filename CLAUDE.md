## Working preferences

Learned from the owner's corrections (Nov 14-15, 2026). One rule per topic; replace a rule instead of adding a contradicting one.

- Report only what exists and was checked; say what was not checked; never present an estimate as a fact.
- Deliver finished files to look at, not explanations of what will be done.
- Decide production, quality and scheduling questions yourself and act (upload, hold, replace, re-render); tell the owner the decision afterwards in one line. Do not ask him. The only things left to him: passwords, purchases, new accounts or terms, deleting a video.
- 2D only (owner, Nov 20 11:13 PM: "Let's keep just 2d cartons on the channel suspend all 3d uploads of songs and fill the spaces with the same carton model we have"): The Animated Zoo uploads only cartoon videos (`studio/cartoon/toon.py`): adventure stories, animal-fact lessons, Guess With Milo and vertical Shorts. Nothing in 3D is built, finished, queued on the PC or uploaded for it, song, lesson or Short; `upload_guard.py` refuses a 3D format, as a fix too, and its audit fails a 3D entry that waits to be uploaded. 3D videos that are public stay (deleting one is the owner's decision); the seven that were scheduled and not public yet were set Private and carry "hold_2d_only" with their ids, and go back on the schedule only if he says so. There is no 2D song video (a voice only speaks, see the rule on notes). Gizmo Explains is 2D only too since Nov 21 (its own rule below). The formats, the cast and the look are in `docs/cartoon/` (plan, bible, findings).
- Fact lessons and adventure stories are at least 5:00 long (6:30 at most). A fact lesson is built from three parts of 85-95 s, each cut whole into one Short of about a minute and a half. A story has a hook in the first 5 s, a goal, three obstacles, a low moment, a solution the characters find, a warm ending, 2-3 true facts shown on screen and a song moment; nobody is hurt, lost, crying or trapped.
- Shorts come from fact lessons and adventure stories, never from songs; each shot is framed again for 9:16, not cropped. Songs have no minimum length: each is as long as it needs.
- Lessons are interactive: questions with answer signs and thinking time, on-screen examples of the exact thing being discussed, every word in sync with the voice. Never just a character moving to narration.
- 4K is the minimum for every video on the channel, cartoon included (owner, Nov 16): 3840x2160, or 2160x3840 for a Short, rendered at that size and not enlarged afterwards. `toon.py` refuses a finished file of any other size (`toon.py --check4k <files>`).
- Every video and every Short gets its own custom thumbnail that shows the example, with 3 words at most and none of the title's words.
- Every video and every Short is uploaded with the channel watermark in the picture (Milo's channel picture + "The Animated Zoo", `studio/zoo3d/watermark.py`); look at the mark's corner sheet: nothing that matters sits under it. Thumbnails carry no mark.
- Daily grid (ET), all cartoon: adventures 5:30 AM, 1:30 PM and 3:30 PM (3:30 PM was the 3D song's slot until Nov 20 11:13 PM); fact lessons and Guess With Milo 9:30 AM and 5:30 PM; Shorts 7:30 AM, 11:30 AM, 7:30 PM. Always schedule, never publish immediately. The cartoon formats were approved by the owner on Nov 16, 3:36 PM ("cartoon formats are approved, build and post"). A slot with no cartoon video ready stays empty: nothing in 3D fills it.
- Uploads: The Animated Zoo is the only channel uploading, so the allowance is all its own (owner, Nov 16): up to 20 uploads in any 24 hours, no lower cap per run. Count the last 24 hours before uploading and before saying anything about a limit with `upload_guard.py` (it counts every video id written into the plans; on a shallow clone `git fetch --deepen=60` first, or it cannot see 24 hours back); stop only when Studio says "Daily upload limit reached".
- Never upload a video twice (owner, Nov 17): one live video per file, per title and per slot. Before every upload `python3 studio/upload_guard.py <plan file>` must say OK (it refuses a file that already has a live id, a title or slot that is already live, a lesson or story on a topic that is already live) and in Studio `zlive("<title>")` on the content list must give live = [] (studio/studio-upload2.js; rows_read 0 = not checked). A fix of a live video is the only second upload: `--replace <old id>`. Record the new id in the plan and commit right after the Studio confirmation, before anything else; `upload_guard.py --audit` at the start of every run.

In Studio's "Add to playlist" dialog never tick a box that shows a dash: it adds the videos already in a second time (Every Episode read 71 for 56 videos). The Home tab is ON with the series shelves; playlist ids, the rules and the dialog's traps are in `docs/PLAYLISTS.md`.

- Before rendering, look at test stills of every example, every answer sign, every quiz answer close-up and every group scene: no character stands inside another, no sign or check mark covers a face or hides behind a character, nothing is cut by the frame or by the Short's crop, each example is the right size next to the animal.

Fix the cause in the engine, re-render, upload the fixed one on the same slot (`upload_guard.py <file> --replace <old id>`), then set the old one to Private and rename it "OLD (replaced): <title>". Old copies do not stay on the channel (owner, Nov 17 11:37 PM: "remove all videos you disabled or turned private ... to organize the channel"): with him there, show him the list once (titles, and that a deletion cannot be undone) and on his yes delete them: content list filtered to Visibility: Private, every row checked against the plan (each id has a live replacement), select all, More actions, Delete forever; afterwards the Private filter reads "No matching videos" on Videos and on Shorts. A run with nobody there leaves them Private and renamed and names them in its report.
- Cartoon videos: the plan check (`toon.py <id> --plan`) must pass before any still is made (no stretch over 6 s without a change, 6 places, 300-390 s, every word placed, the story beats); `pace_check.py --max 8` and `watermark.py check` must pass on the finished file.
- Cartoon stills: look at one still of EVERY shot, in 16:9 and again in 9:16, before rendering (`studio/cartoon/stills.sh`), and again after each fix. Each look so far found defects the plan check cannot see: a word on a face or on the watermark, half a character at the frame edge, a caption on feet, a nose that stayed open on "Closed!", words stacked on one spot in 9:16, a strip of another colour at the top or bottom of a tall 9:16 view.
- Cartoon Shorts: every shot of a Short has its own 9:16 camera (`pcam`, and `pcam2` for wide views so the slow zoom never cuts a character); the title band is two lines of at most 15 and 10 letters; a lesson's parts are written 83-90 s long so part + hook is a Short of 85-95 s.
- A walk-in is `move` (where the actor starts) + `("walk", at, who, dx, seconds)`; a `fromto` on x together with a walk of 0 leaves the actor at the start (seen in zc_ep02's stills, Nov 16).
- In toon.py a group the engine moves carries only a translate; its size and its mirror go on a group inside it (GSAP measures x and y of a scaled SVG group from its bounding box: a tween to x = 300 stopped at 257).
- No voice is synthesized onto notes in a cartoon: no sung voice, no hummed voice (owner, Nov 17 8:25 PM, on za_ep02: "when Milo starts singing it sounds robotic ... sounds should be clean and musical if music is introduced"). A voice only SPEAKS. Music is played by instruments: a song moment is the rhyme SAID by the character in an ordinary spoken shot, then the tune played by the bells and the soft band in the next shot (`sing=` whose line text is only `~` words, one per note group, written notes in C at 96, entering on a beat, the words on screen with numeric times), never `"hum": True`, never lyrics in a `sing` line. An animal that hums or calls gets the same: its line named in speech, then its tune on instruments in its own shot; never a sound effect on top of the narration (owner, Nov 16: the camels' two-note beep). A tune's notes over a chord are that chord's notes or a whole step from them (an E held over the F bar clashed, Nov 17). `listen.py --episode <id>` is run BEFORE the render (it needs no picture) and must show no click and no clash inside a tune: the first re-render of za_ep01/02 had clicks from bell notes cut short, found only after 90 minutes of rendering. listen.py cannot tell robotic from musical: a new kind of sound is sent to the owner to hear before it is used in a video.
- Cartoon music and sync follow the 3D process (owner, Nov 16 9:40 PM): every new episode has `"beat": True` in its EP: the 3D lessons' music bed lowered under the voice the same way, every cut on the music's half-beat, sung shots entering on a beat, idle motion in tempo, the mouth read at every frame, and teach.py's word-sync checks in the plan (`sync`: all cuts on the half-beat, all songs on a beat, no suspect word). Episodes rendered before that keep their file without it, so their timing can be rebuilt.

- Pronunciation (owner, Nov 20 12:44 PM: "Where does the camel live?" was read like "five"): `sing.say()` sends the verb "live"/"lives" to the voice as "liv"/"livs" (/lɪv/) through `fix_pron()`; captions and titles keep the spelling. A new mispronounced word goes into `fix_pron()` with a phonemize check (`kokoro().tokenizer.phonemize(text, "en-us")`), never a respelling in the script.
- A cue on a word that is said twice needs its number ("w:Closed!:2"); punctuation does not tell the two apart.
