# Viral 3D sing-along playbook (v1, Nov 4, 2026). The daily views check updates this from real numbers.

## The format
- **One 3D episode per channel per day.** Each runs 2:30 to 3:30 and is a sing-along song that teaches one simple idea.
- Structure: HOOK (first 5 s: the host pops in, names the topic, and something fun happens) -> verse 1 -> CHORUS ->
  spoken fact (one short sentence) -> verse 2 -> CHORUS -> action break ("clap, clap! jump, jump!") -> verse 3 ->
  CHORUS, sung twice, the last time with more energy and every character joining in -> 5 s outro ("Sing it again tomorrow!").
- The chorus comes back at least 3 times and is 4 lines long, with its last line equal to the first or nearly the same.
- The chorus title phrase is the video title (for example "Fire Truck Song"). Kids search the song, so name it.

## Songwriting rules for viral kids songs
- 4 chords (I V vi IV or I IV V I), bpm 100-125 for daytime channels and 66-80 for Moonbeam.
- Melody range is a 6th at most (C4-A4). Mostly stepwise, with repeated notes and short phrases. Every line is
  sing-able by a 3-year-old.
- Repetition is the hook: repeated words ("Wee-oo, wee-oo!", "Count with me: one, two, three!"), sound effects,
  call and response (host sings, kids echo), and the kids' names for things.
- Concrete, visual teaching: colors, counting, shapes, opposites, how machines work, animal and vehicle sounds, healthy
  habits, bedtime routines. One idea per song, repeated in every verse with new examples.
- Action words the viewer can do: clap, stomp, spin, wave, jump, point, yawn.
- Words: 1-2 syllables, no rhymes that make no sense. Facts must be TRUE and safe (no dangerous imitation:
  never touch fire, wires, medicine, stoves; always with a grown-up).

## Visual rules
- A close-up of the host's face is never more than 10 s away. Faces drive watch time.
- Cut on every sung line (shots cycle). Use wide shots for groups and dancing, close-ups for singing, and low/orbit
  shots for the big chorus.
- Pop-in 3D words, numbers and colors teach the idea on screen (props). Counting songs show the number as each one is sung.
- Every character dances on the beat. The big final chorus uses energy 1.0, wave and jump.
- Bright saturated palette, warm sunny light (Moonbeam: cozy moonlight, soft glow, slow and gentle).

## Packaging (yt-package / yt-seo)
- Title: "<Topic> Song | <Channel> Kids Songs & Nursery Rhymes", 60 characters or less, with the song name first.
- The thumbnail is the host's face, big and happy, plus the topic object and 1-3 bold words.
- Description: the first 2 lines give the song name, what kids learn and the ages (2-6). Then the lyrics, which help search.
