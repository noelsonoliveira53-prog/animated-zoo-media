## YouTube description (under 5,000 characters, no '<' or '>', no AI line)
Whose feet are these? A giant footprint in the sand starts the mystery. Guess with Milo the bluebird, then meet the animal: an elephant walks on its tiptoes on a soft cushion, a giraffe has two hooves on each foot and walks this side, that side, and a zebra walks on just one toe inside each hoof.
