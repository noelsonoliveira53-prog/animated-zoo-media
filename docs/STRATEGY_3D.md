# Viral 3D sing-along playbook (v2, Nov 5, 2026). The daily views check updates this from real numbers.

## The format
- **One 3D episode per channel per day.** Each runs 2:30 to 3:30 and is a sing-along song that teaches one simple idea.
- Structure: HOOK (first 5 s: the host pops in, names the topic, and something fun happens) -> verse 1 -> CHORUS ->
  spoken fact (one short sentence) -> verse 2 -> CHORUS -> action break ("clap, clap! jump, jump!") -> verse 3 ->
  CHORUS, sung twice, the last time with more energy and every character joining in -> 5 s outro ("Sing it again tomorrow!").
- The chorus comes back at least 3 times and is 4 lines long, with its last line equal to the first or nearly the same.
- The chorus title phrase is the video title (for example "Fire Truck Song"). Kids search the song, so name it.

## Songwriting rules for viral kids songs
- 4 chords (I V vi IV or I IV V I), bpm 100-125 for daytime channels and 66-80 for Moonbeam.
- Melody range is a 6th at most (C4-A4). Mostly stepwise, with repeated notes and short phrases. Every line is
  sing-able by a 3-year-old.
- Repetition is the hook: repeated words ("Wee-oo, wee-oo!", "Count with me: one, two, three!"), sound effects,
  call and response (host sings, kids echo), and the kids' names for things.
- Concrete, visual teaching: colors, counting, shapes, opposites, how machines work, animal and vehicle sounds, healthy
  habits, bedtime routines. One idea per song, repeated in every verse with new examples.
- Action words the viewer can do: clap, stomp, spin, wave, jump, point, yawn.
- Words: 1-2 syllables, no rhymes that make no sense. Facts must be TRUE and safe (no dangerous imitation:
  never touch fire, wires, medicine, stoves; always with a grown-up).

## Visual rules
- A close-up of the host's face is never more than 10 s away. Faces drive watch time.
- Cut on every sung line (shots cycle). Use wide shots for groups and dancing, close-ups for singing, and low/orbit
  shots for the big chorus.
- Pop-in 3D words, numbers and colors teach the idea on screen (props). Counting songs show the number as each one is sung.
- Every character dances on the beat. The big final chorus uses energy 1.0, wave and jump.
- Bright saturated palette, warm sunny light (Moonbeam: cozy moonlight, soft glow, slow and gentle).

## Packaging (yt-package / yt-seo)
- Title (changed Nov 5): "<Topic> Song for Kids! <number or hook> with <Host>", 60 characters or less, song name first.
  Example: "Colors Song for Kids! 6 Colors with Bloop". The best CTRs so far are "Meet 5 Zoo Friends" (3.6%) and
  "Goodnight 5 Sleepy Animals 🌙" (5.5%), so a number and a friendly noun belong in the title. One theme emoji at the end is allowed.
  The old "| <Channel> Kids Songs & Nursery Rhymes" suffix is dropped: it spends characters on words nobody clicks.
- The thumbnail is the host's face, big and happy, plus the topic object and 1-3 bold words.
- Description: the first 2 lines give the song name, what kids learn and the ages (2-6). Then the lyrics, which help search.

## Evidence so far (Nov 4)
- The Animated Zoo's "Savanna Animals for Kids! Meet 5 Zoo Friends with Milo" (5 min) reached 327 views in 2 days, about 5x anything else.
  Follow its pattern: "<Topic> for Kids!" + a number + the host's name; one clear theme; long-form.
- (Superseded Nov 5) Do not stretch songs to 4-5 min yet; see below.

## Adjustments from the Nov 5 views check (all 6 channels)
- **Hook rule (new, most important):** viewers leave after 13-42 s on every 5-minute episode (5-14% viewed). The chorus's first
  line and the most visual moment must land inside the first 10 s. No title card, no "Hi friends" warm-up longer than 3 s.
  Ocean & Ice, whose first animal and first fact arrive fastest, holds viewers longest (0:42) and grew 3.5x faster than Savanna today.
- **Length:** keep every 3D song at 2:30-3:30 (target 3:00). Only add a verse once average view duration passes 1:00.
- **Fact early:** the first spoken fact comes right after the first chorus (by about 0:30), as a "Did you know?" with a pop-in prop.
- **Format:** long-form only. Shorts got 4-70 impressions each and almost no views, so keeping them dropped is right.
- **Posting times:** no signal yet (too few uploads). Keep each channel's slot (Truck 1:40, Bloop 1:50, Gizmo 2:10, Fruity 2:20,
  Moonbeam 7:00 PM, Zoo 5:00 PM ET) for at least a week so timing can be compared.
- **Watch next check:** the first 3D uploads (Bloop Colors Song, Fruity Yummy Fruits Song, both Nov 5) vs. the 2D episodes on the same
  channels (Bloop 2D: 2.4% CTR, 0:27 average view).

## Adjustments from the Nov 6 views check (first 3D numbers)
- **3D songs are working: keep going and keep them short.** Early 3D watch time is 0:47-1:08 (24-41% viewed) vs 0:13-0:45 (5-15%) for 2D.
  The 2:31 Bloop cut kept the most (40.7%). New target length: **2:30-3:00** (hard max 3:30). Cut a verse before adding one.
- **One theme per song.** Mixed lists lose viewers: "Amazing Animal Bodies" (mixed facts) holds 0:18 vs 0:45 for one-habitat Ocean & Ice.
  Every song is one habitat, one vehicle, one routine, or one concept (colors, counting, weather).
- **Zoo topic queue (proven habitats first):** Ocean Animals Song, Jungle/Treetop Animals Song, Polar Animals Song, then Farm and Baby Animals.
  The Zoo gets 95% of network impressions, so give it the first finished render each day.
- **Titles:** keep "<Topic> Song for Kids! <number + friendly noun> with <Host>". Prefer a number ("Count 1 to 5", "5 Ocean Friends")
  over "Surprise Facts" (1.6-2.3% CTR vs 3.7% for "Meet 5 Zoo Friends"). Moonbeam titles end with 🌙 (5.9% CTR, best in the network).
- **Posting times:** still no signal; keep the slots (Truck 1:40, Bloop 1:50, Gizmo 2:10, Fruity 2:20, Zoo 4:00 3D / 5:00 2D, Moonbeam 7:00 PM ET).
- **Next check:** compare the six 3D songs that air Nov 5-7 once each has a day of impressions (CTR vs the 2D episodes on the same channel).
