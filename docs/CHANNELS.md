## Formats

- **Kids Short**: vertical 1080x1920, 20-60 s, 6-8 scenes, about 100-125 words of narration.
- **Kids long video**: horizontal 1920x1080, 4:30-6:30, 30-40 scenes, about 600-720 words.
- **Dog TV long-form**: 1920x1080, length rotates 1, 3, 5 hours. One brand-new ~2-minute instrumental track per video, crossfaded into a seamless loop, at -20 LUFS (peaks below -3 dBTP), over 10 different scenery clips (2.5-3.5 min each, fade between), looped to full length.

## Writing rules

Kids scripts: warm, playful, simple words, short sentences, gentle repetition and questions to the viewer. Every video teaches one small true thing. Strong first 2 seconds, no clickbait. Titles in Title Case, under 60 characters, at most one emoji.

Never in kids content: danger or fear, anyone hurt/lost/crying/trapped (even if rescued), acts kids could copy that are unsafe, real people, other companies' characters, brands or songs, false facts, gross-out humor, calls to action aimed at kids.

Dog TV metadata: honest (original calm track gently looped over real scenery), title states the exact length ("3 Hours"), no medical claims ("helps dogs relax" is fine, "cures anxiety" is not).

Descriptions end with the channel boilerplate:

- kids: "{name} makes gentle, colorful videos for little learners. New videos every day!

Made with care for ages {audience_short}. Characters and visuals are animated with the help of AI tools and reviewed for kid safety before publishing."
- dogtv: "{name}: calming TV for dogs. Leave this on while you're out to help your dog relax.

Music is an original track composed for this video with AI and gently looped. Scenery is licensed stock footage. Turn the volume to a low, comfortable level."

Banned in titles and descriptions: cocomelon, bluey, peppa, paw patrol, disney, pixar, pokemon, minecraft, spiderman, spider-man, elsa, mickey, sesame, blippi, ms rachel, baby shark, pinkfong, you won't believe, gone wrong, scary, died, blood, kill.

## Rulebook (every video must pass)

- **CAD-01 Daily quota met**: Each channel fills every slot its ramp week calls for. New channels ramp up over 4 weeks instead of starting at full speed.
- **CAD-02 Spacing between uploads**: At least 2 hours between two uploads on the same channel.
- **CAD-03 Network upload cap**: No more than 90 uploads per day across the network (YouTube allows 100 upload calls per day per API project).
- **KID-01 Made for Kids set**: Every kids upload is declared Made for Kids, and the manager confirms YouTube shows it that way after upload.
- **KID-02 Safety review passed**: Claude reviews every script before render: no violence, scary scenes, dangerous acts kids could copy, real people, other brands' characters, or false facts.
- **KID-03 No distress storylines**: No animals or children hurt, lost, crying or in danger, even if rescued later. YouTube demonetizes distress content.
- **KID-04 Length in range**: Shorts 20-60 s. Long videos 4:30-6:30.
- **DOG-01 Fresh track every video**: Each video gets its own newly composed 2-minute track. A track is never reused.
- **DOG-02 Seamless loop**: Track is 110-130 s and crossfaded at the loop point, so there is no click or gap when it repeats.
- **DOG-03 Calm loudness**: Audio sits at -20 LUFS (±2) with peaks under -3 dBTP and no sudden jumps at the loop seam.
- **DOG-04 Visual variety**: At least 8 different scenes per video, and no clip is reused on the same channel within 30 days.
- **DOG-05 Length matches title**: Video is 1, 3 or 5 hours, and the title states the real length.
- **DOG-06 Not Made for Kids**: Dog TV is for dogs and their owners, so it is declared not Made for Kids.
- **META-01 Clean title**: Title is 70 characters or less, has at most one ALL-CAPS word (short ones like ABC are fine), and no other brands' names or misleading claims.
- **META-02 Unique title**: No title repeats on the same channel within 90 days.
- **META-03 Disclosure in description**: Description includes the channel boilerplate and says when music or visuals were made with AI.
- **OPS-01 Failures retried**: A failed job is retried up to 2 more times (10 min, then 45 min later), then raised as an incident.
- **OPS-02 Upload verified**: Within 2 hours of publish time, the manager confirms the video exists, finished processing, and went public.
- **OPS-03 Channel sign-in healthy**: Each channel's YouTube sign-in token refreshes successfully.
- **OPS-04 Auto-pause on rejection**: If YouTube rejects or removes a video, that channel pauses itself and you get an alert.
