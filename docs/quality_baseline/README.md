# Minimum quality bar (approved by Noelson, Nov 14, 2026)
Noelson on these two sheets: "I like these, let's keep minimal quality at this level and improve from this."
Every new episode (song or lesson) must look at least this good, and each one should be a little better than the last.
What the baseline shows, and what the review compares against:
- each scene is a different place with its own landmark (barn and silo, rock arch and cactus, rainbow meadow, beach umbrella, cabin, bamboo);
- the animal a line is about is alone with Milo, big and close, not a far line-up of six;
- warm, varied light (noon, golden hour, sunset) and a clear sky gradient;
- clean on-model characters with readable faces; the 3D word on screen is readable and not cut off.
A review contact sheet that looks flatter, emptier or more repetitive than these is a FAIL: fix the scene plan and re-render.
When a later episode is clearly better, add its sheet here as the new baseline (keep the old ones).
